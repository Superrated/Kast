How it works

One visit to measure, one day to hang. Most rooms are done within three weeks.

  1. Measure

    We play a test sweep and record how long the room rings at every pitch. About an hour.

  2. Design

    You get a drawing of your wall or ceiling with the panels on it, and the expected result.

  3. Make

    We make the panels in our workshop in Kortrijk, to the centimetre of your room.

  4. Hang

    One day on site for most rooms. No dust: everything is cut before we arrive.

  5. Measure again

    The same test after hanging. You get both graphs, before and after.

Questions

Will panels stop noise from the neighbours?

No. Panels stop echo inside the room. Keeping sound out of a room is soundproofing, which means building work. We'll tell you honestly if that's what you need.

Are the panels fire-safe?

For restaurants, offices and other public spaces we only use materials with an official fire rating (at least B-s2, d0) and give you the certificates. Oak slats for public spaces get a fire-retardant treatment.

How are they fixed?

Screwed to the wall or hung from the ceiling on cables. They come off again without damage, apart from a few screw holes.

Why measure? Can't you just guess?

A guess puts too much or too little on the wall. Measuring shows which pitches ring, where, and how much panel you really need. You pay for less panel, not more.

Which area do you cover?

Kortrijk and the rest of West and East Flanders. Further away on request, with a travel cost by distance.
