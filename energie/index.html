How it works

From your first call to a working battery in about three weeks, most of it waiting for the inspection.

  1. Send your numbers

    A photo of your inverter and your yearly use from the Fluvius app. We tell you if a battery pays off for you.

  2. Home visit

    We check the fuse box and where the battery can go, and give a fixed price.

  3. Fitting

    Usually one day. Set up in the app, tested, and explained.

  4. Paperwork

    AREI inspection booked and the report to Fluvius made for you.

Questions

Is there still a premium for a battery?

No, Flanders stopped it. You do get 6% VAT instead of 21% if your home is older than 10 years and an installer fits it.

Does a battery pay off?

Usually in 8 to 15 years, depending on how much sun you have left over. We tell you honestly if it doesn't for you.

Why does it need an inspection?

A battery, a new inverter and a car charger all change your installation, so an AREI inspection and a report to Fluvius are required. We arrange both.

What warranty do I get?

The maker's warranty on the battery and inverter (usually 10 years) and 2 years on our work.

Can my broken inverter be repaired instead?

Sometimes, and then it's cheaper. We check that first and tell you.
