# BRUIS Energie (prototype)

Elektro grown into a full trade: home batteries, inverter swaps and EV chargers, with the AREI inspection and Fluvius report arranged.

- **What a battery saves**: panel size, battery size and current inverter give savings per year, payback and the fitted price.
- **Prices** (incl. 6% VAT, homes older than 10 years): battery 5 kWh with hybrid inverter €3,750, 10 kWh €6,500, battery only from €3,000, inverter swap €1,400, car charger from €1,300.
- How it works, FAQ, quote form. Prototype: nothing is sold, booked or sent.

One file: `index.html`.
