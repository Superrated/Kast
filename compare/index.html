    All the numbers

    Prices and costs come from the five financial plans. Sales come from comparable companies, not from the plans (BRUIS — what comparable companies show). Marketing: the first euros win a sale at the cost seen in public benchmarks; each next euro wins a bit less. "The budget that works" keeps spending while one more sale still leaves you at least half its money.

    Home: as a sole trader you can't rent to yourself, so BRUIS carries the business share of your home costs (loan interest, insurance, property tax by floor area; energy by the workshop meter) and pays the workshop upgrades; with a BV you could charge it real rent. Van: a light van is up to 100% deductible when used only for the business; private use lowers that.

    Tax: social contributions for a side business and income tax at the top rate on top of a job. All figures are estimates to check with your accountant.
