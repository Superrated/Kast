# BRUIS Elektro (prototype)

A one-page site for replacing broken solar inverters at a fixed price, with the report to Fluvius and the AREI inspection arranged.

- **What it costs you**: installation size and season give the money lost per day and month; price for the same kind of inverter or a hybrid.
- **Prices** (incl. 6% VAT, homes older than 10 years): up to 3.6 kW €1,400, 4–6 kW €1,600, hybrid from €2,300, diagnosis €95 (taken off the price).
- How it works, FAQ, report form with photo. Prototype: nothing is sold, booked or sent.

One file: `index.html`.
