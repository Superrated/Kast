How it works

Most inverters are replaced within a week of your call. The work itself takes about two hours.

  1. Send a photo

    Of the display or the label. We tell you by phone if it's the inverter and what it will cost.

  2. Diagnosis

    We measure the panels and the inverter on site, so you only replace what's broken.

  3. Replace

    A new inverter from SMA, Fronius or SolarEdge, fitted and tested. Your monitoring app set up again.

  4. Paperwork

    We report the change to Fluvius and book the AREI inspection. You get the certificate.

Questions

Is it really the inverter, not the panels?

In most cases, yes: panels last 25 years or more, inverters usually 10 to 15. We measure both before we replace anything.

Why does it need an inspection?

A replaced inverter must be reported to Fluvius and the installation needs a new AREI inspection. We arrange both; the cost is in the price.

What warranty do I get?

The maker's warranty on the inverter (usually 5 to 10 years, longer with registration) and 2 years on our work.

Can I add a home battery later?

Choose the hybrid inverter and a battery can be connected later without changing the inverter again.

Do you do other electrical work?

Yes: fixing the points from a failed electrical inspection, extra circuits, sockets and lights, planned on weekdays after hours or on Saturdays. No emergency call-outs.
