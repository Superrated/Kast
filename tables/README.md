# BRUIS Tables (prototype)

A one-page site for modern oak dining tables made from old solid oak tables.

- **Make your table**: shape (rectangle, rounded, pill, round), length 160–280 cm or a 110–150 cm circle, width 85/95/105 cm, legs (oak blocks or oak trestles; no metal), five Rubio Monocoat oils, and "make it from my own old table" (€ 350 off). The drawing shows the table from the side and from above, board by board; "Show other boards" deals a new set.
- **Price**: € 590 + € 700 per m² of top, + shape and legs, rounded to € 10; delivery € 95 in Belgium or collect free.
- **Your old table**: what we look for and what we pay (€ 50–150) or take off (€ 350).
- **Table passports**: example cards for where the wood came from.
- Forms are a prototype: nothing is sent.

One file: `index.html`. The business plan and roadbook are separate docs.
