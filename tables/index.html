How a table is made

About six weeks from your order to your door. We make one or two tables at a time.

  1. FindA solid oak table, at least 160 cm long, from a second-hand site, a clearance or your own home.
  2. Take apartOld finish off, screws and nails out. We keep the boards and, where they are good, the legs.
  3. PlaneEvery board planed back to bright oak. Old dowel holes and a few saw marks stay as small marks of history.
  4. Glue and shapeThe boards are glued into a new top, made flat, cut to your size and shape, with steel strips underneath to keep it straight.
  5. OilTwo coats of Rubio Monocoat hardwax oil. Stains wipe off, and a scratch can be sanded and oiled again at home.

Questions

Is it really solid oak?

Yes. We only take tables with solid oak tops. After planing the top is about 3.5 cm thick, the same as a new solid oak table.

Will I see the old table in it?

A little. The boards are bright and new on the surface, but you may find a filled dowel hole, a slightly darker board or a knot. No two tables look the same.

Will the top warp?

Old oak has dried for decades, so it moves less than new oak. We still fit steel strips under every top.

How do I look after it?

Wipe it with a damp cloth. Once or twice a year, a little Rubio maintenance oil. Scratches can be sanded and oiled locally.

Why no metal legs?

Because the whole table can come from old oak: the legs of the old table often become the new legs or trestles. Oak legs can also be sanded and oiled again, like the top.

Where do you deliver?

All of Belgium, ourselves: no courier. The price goes by distance from the workshop, € 45 up to 30 km, € 85 up to 80 km, € 135 up to 150 km and € 185 beyond. Two of us carry it in and set it up. Wait for the monthly route day in your region and it is € 30 less, or collect it from the workshop for free.

Can I come and see the wood first?

Yes, by appointment. We send you photos of the boards before we glue the top.
