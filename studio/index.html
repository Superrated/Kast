How a month works

Everything is batched, so you answer us once a month instead of every day.

  1. Plan

    A 30-minute talk: what's coming up, what sold, what you want to push.

  2. Make

    We make the month's posts, newsletter and ads from your templates. AI writes drafts and variations; the designer picks, fixes and finishes.

  3. Check

    You get everything in one go and ask for changes, twice if you need to.

  4. Publish

    We schedule it all. At the end of the month you see what worked.
