The four objects

Each batch is small. When it's gone, the next one is cast within about two weeks.

How they're made

Three materials, each doing what it's best at.

Concrete

Fine concrete with pigment, poured into silicone moulds, cured for a week and sealed. Natural grey, charcoal or sand. Every piece has its own small air holes.

Old oak

Offcuts from the old oak tables we turn into new ones. Sanded, oiled with Rubio Monocoat and felted underneath.

3D print

Our own shapes, printed in our workshop: the lamp shade, and the masters for every mould. No downloaded designs.

Every piece has a passport

A small card in the box says which table the oak came from, when it was cast and which batch it belongs to. The same card tells you how to use it safely.

Care and safety

The same information is on the card in every box.

Candle holder

Never leave burning candles alone. Keep 10 cm between candles and away from anything that can burn. Put it on a heat-proof surface.

Lamp

Charge with the USB-C cable that comes with it. The LED module is CE-marked. Keep the shade away from heat. For indoor use.

Plant pot

Sealed inside, with a drainage hole. Put it on its oak saucer. Not for food.

Bookends

About 1.5 kg each. Felt underneath protects your shelf. Dust with a dry cloth; no cleaning products.

Questions

Why do the pieces look slightly different?

Concrete is poured by hand, so every piece has its own air holes and shading, and every bit of oak has its own grain. That is part of it.

How much is postage?

€7 by post in Belgium, free from €100. Picking up in Heule is always free.

Can I return something?

Yes, within 14 days of receiving it, unused and in its box. Pieces with a company logo can't be returned.

Something broke in the post.

Send us a photo within 48 hours and we send a new one.

Do you ship outside Belgium?

Not yet from this site. You can find us on Etsy for other countries.
