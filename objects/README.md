# BRUIS Objects (prototype)

A one-page shop for four home objects made in small batches from cast concrete, old oak offcuts and 3D-printed parts.

- **Objects**: candle holder (€29), plant pot (€45), cordless lamp (€95), bookends (€55). Concrete in natural grey, charcoal or sand.
- **Shop**: small batches with places left, a bag with pick-up in Heule (free) or post in Belgium (€7, free from €100).
- **Story**: how they're made, a passport card per piece, markets, company gifts and wholesale, care and safety (product safety rules), FAQ.
- Prototype: nothing is sold, paid or sent.

One file: `index.html`.
