Safe, and still real

Children use real hand tools, sized for them, with two of us in the room. Adults also use a cordless drill and a sander. The big saws stay ours.

  • Two grown-ups for children's workshops, one for every four children.
  • Hand tools for children: pull saws, hand drills, hammers, rasps and sandpaper. No machines.
  • Glasses and ear protection in every size, and long hair tied back.
  • A first-aid kit, a fire extinguisher and a free way out, checked before every workshop.
  • Insured: every participant is covered by our liability insurance.
  • Photos only with your yes: we ask parents before a child is in any picture.

Questions

From what age?

Children's workshops are for 7 to 12 years. In the family workshops children from 5 can join with a parent. Adult workshops are from 16.

Do parents stay?

For children's workshops you can drop them off; we have two grown-ups in the room. For family workshops you work together.

Do I need to bring anything?

Clothes that can get dusty and closed shoes. Tools, wood, glasses, aprons and drinks are there. For a full day, bring your lunch.

What if I can't come?

Up to 7 days before, you get your money back. After that, send someone in your place or take a voucher for another date.

Where is it?

In our workshop in Heule, Kortrijk (8501), with parking in the street. The full address comes with your booking.
