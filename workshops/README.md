# BRUIS Workshops (prototype)

A one-page site for woodworking workshops in the BRUIS workshop in Heule (Kortrijk, 8501): for children (7–12), a parent with a child, and adults.

- **Workshops**: Build a birdhouse and A treasure box (children, 2½ h, € 39), A stool, together (parent and child, 3 h, € 85 a duo), Oak from old tables (adults, a day, € 155), Your own side table (adults, a weekend, € 340). Each card draws what you take home.
- **Dates**: example dates in 2027 with places left, a booking window (number of places, names, total) and a waiting list.
- **Parties and groups**: kids' birthday € 240, team day € 900, family day € 340, gift vouchers.
- **Safety**: two adults for children's workshops, hand tools only for children, glasses and ear protection, first aid, insurance, photos only with consent.
- Forms are a prototype: nothing is booked, paid or sent.

One file: `index.html`.
