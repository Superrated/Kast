Why active?

Each driver gets its own amplifier and the DSP does the crossover. You need no separate amplifier, and the speaker is tuned exactly for its drivers and box.

Can I stream to them?

Plug in any streamer, a turntable with a phono stage, or a TV through the optical input. We keep wireless out of the speaker so it stays simple and lasts.

Twee or Twee Reference?

Twee uses compact class D boards and an external power adapter. Reference uses Hypex FusionAmp modules with more power and finer DSP, for bigger rooms and louder listening.

Is it safe and legal?

Every pair carries the CE mark, a Recupel take-back contribution and a label with our name, address and batch number. The Twee runs on a certified 24 V adapter, so no mains voltage enters the speaker.
